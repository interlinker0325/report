## 2. How Many tokens are Suitable for Sniping (Just Trading)?

Here, perhaps only 1% to 5% of the thousands of daily tokens are viable sniping targets.

## 3. So what is the others and how many are those?

### The others
- Test tokens: People just learning how to deploy.
- Joke tokens: Made for a friend or a small group.
- Instant rug pulls: Tokens created with the intent to drain liquidity immediately.
- "Soft rugs": Tokens where the developer abandons the project shortly after launch.

### How Many Are Rug Pull Tokens? 
This is the darkest part of the ecosystem. The percentage is alarmingly high.

- On Hyper-Deployer Chains: It is estimated that over 80% to 95% of tokens launched on platforms like Pump.fun end up being some form of a rug pull or an abandoned project.
- Hard Rug: Developer removes all liquidity, and the token price goes to zero. This is less common now on platforms with bonding curves (like Pump.fun) but still happens after the token migrates to a DEX.
- Soft Rug / Slow Rug: The developer stops marketing, abandons the social channels, and sells their holdings slowly, causing the price to bleed to zero.

The goal of a sniper is to identify that tiny fraction of tokens that have a real team, a growing community, and a plan before they gain massive traction.

## Overall guide to classify the rag tokens and safe tokens(tokens can use the sniping)

| Aspect   | 🚩 Rug Token (AVOID)     | ✅ Safe Token (CONSIDER)    |
| ------------ | -----------: | :----------: |
| Liquidity Pool   | Not locked, or locked for a few days  | Locked for 6+ months on a verified site |
| Holder Distribution | Top wallet holds 30%+ of supply | Supply is well-distributed |
| Creator Wallet    | History of past rug pulls        | Clean history or doxxed team      |
| Mint Authority    | Not renounced (can create more tokens)        | Renounced (fixed supply)      |
| Social Community    | Bots, spam, no real engagement        | Active, organic discussion, AMAs      |
| Website/Plan    | Generic, copy-pasted, no roadmap        | Professional, unique, clear roadmap      |
